1. Who we are

This website is operated by [Company legal name] ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains how we handle personal data relating to businesses and their representatives (merchants, authorised resellers, platform partners and developers) who visit the business pages of this website.

Contact: [Company legal name], [registered address], [registration number]. Email: [privacy contact email].

2. Scope and current status

This website is a pre-launch preview. It describes services that are in development and not yet available. Today the website does not ask you to create an account, does not take payments, and its forms do not send or store what you type.

When we launch any service, or start collecting information through a form, we will update this policy before that collection begins and tell you what changes.

3. Personal data we process

Technical data. When you visit, our hosting provider automatically receives technical information needed to deliver the pages, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the page requested, the referring page and the date and time of the request.

Preferences stored on your device. The website may save small settings in your browser (for example, which menu layout you chose) using browser storage. These stay on your device and are not sent to us. See our Cookie Policy.

Information you send us directly. If you email us, we process your email address, name and the content of your message.

Business contact data. If you correspond with us about a pilot, partnership or developer access, we process your name, job title, company, business contact details and the content of our correspondence.

4. Why we use it and our legal basis

  • To deliver and secure the website, prevent abuse and fix faults (our legitimate interests in running a safe, working website).
  • To reply to messages you send us (to take steps you ask for, or our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries). To assess and manage business relationships, pilots and partnerships.
  • To comply with legal obligations and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Where the law requires consent, we will ask for it first and you may withdraw it at any time. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal data only as needed with: our website hosting and content delivery provider; Google, which serves the website fonts and receives your IP address when your browser requests them; professional advisers such as lawyers and accountants; and authorities where the law requires. Our service providers act on our instructions and must protect the data.

6. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside the country where you are located, including in the United States. Where we transfer personal data internationally, we take steps required by applicable law, such as contractual safeguards, so that it receives a comparable standard of protection.

7. How long we keep it

Hosting logs are kept for the period set by our hosting provider for security and operations. Correspondence is kept for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any resulting business relationship, and then for any period required by law.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 and, where it applies, the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, contact us using the details above. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the data involved. No transmission over the internet is completely secure.

10. Children

This website is not directed at children under 13 and the business pages are intended for business users only. We do not knowingly collect their personal data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Material changes will be highlighted on this website.
