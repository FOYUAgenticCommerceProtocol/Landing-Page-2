How every order is decided

Whichever way you connect, the same chain of authority applies. No call and no assistant can skip a step.

  1. The person sets the limitsA shopper signs their limits with a passkey on their own device.
  2. The policy engine decidesEach order is checked against those limits: it goes ahead, waits for the person's yes, or is refused, with the reason.
  3. The payment provider authorisesPayment is authorised by the bank or payment provider, never by an assistant.
  4. The store stays the sellerThe store accepts the order and keeps the customer relationship.

Preview and access

The servers and the API are in a private test. Accounts are by invitation, orders are test orders, and the sandbox uses fictional stores.
